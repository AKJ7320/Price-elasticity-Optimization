# Data and model chronology

- Training data is synthetic, not observed business data. The generator retains its original seed, distributions, 10,800 SKUs, 26 week indices, prices, units, costs, and ground-truth elasticities.
- The formerly undated week indices 0–25 now map to Mondays from 2025-06-30 through 2025-12-22. The final interval ends 2025-12-28, before every reconstructed project commit. No recorded historical observation was shifted from another year.
- Per-SKU recommendations and annual business-impact figures are illustrative 2026 scenarios based on the final training week. Weekly units/profit in the optimization output are not annual forecasts; the annual business-impact calculation uses separate stated assumptions.
- Calendar labels do not enter model features: the seasonal functions still use the unchanged week index. Regenerated metrics and numeric optimization results are checked against the original snapshot.
- Ground-truth elasticities are not passed to fitting, but belong to the same synthetic SKUs being estimated. Evaluation is recovery of synthetic parameters, not an out-of-time demand forecast test. The 100 − MAPE score is not classification accuracy.
- All four figures are regenerated by `python src/train.py` under `screenshots/`. They show the 2025 training window; forward-looking price/profit plots are explicitly labeled as 2026 scenarios.
- The existing monolithic project was reorganized into six logical commits with the requested January–February 2026 dates. This reconstructs file progression; it does not establish when the original work was performed.
